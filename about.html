BIOGRAPHY & ENGINEERING ETHOS

Building the Future from Rajdhanwar, Jharkhand

I am a 14-year-old student, software engineer, and young founder driven by deep curiosity, mathematical discipline, and a passion for turning complex problems into elegant, real-world software.

My Story

My programming journey began not in a Silicon Valley accelerator, but from my desk in Rajdhanwar, Jharkhand. While pursuing my education at Manthan Valley ST. Albert Senior Secondary School, I spent countless late nights teaching myself data structures, digital signal processing algorithms, and machine learning architectures.

Rather than building toy projects, I focus on shipping production software. At 14, I founded Spark2Ship to empower student builders to transform rough ideas into structured code architectures, engineered HoloVerse AI (procedural 3D world synthesis with zero-hardware webcam MoCap), and built sabhyamai.in, an AI virtual try-on engine combining computer vision and generative models.

I believe that age is merely a number when matched with unrelenting grit, intellectual honesty, and deep technical curiosity.
